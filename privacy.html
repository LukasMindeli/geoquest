Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information WorldOrWrong may process when you play, sign in with Google, use chat, or appear on leaderboards.

1. Data we may process

  • Google account data you choose to share through Firebase Authentication, such as your display name, avatar, and account identifier.
  • Gameplay data such as scores, streaks, achievements, leaderboard entries, and profile progress.
  • Chat messages you submit inside the public chat.
  • Technical data stored locally in your browser, such as language choice and game progress.

2. How the data is used

  • To let you sign in and keep a profile linked to your progress.
  • To run leaderboards, multiplayer features, achievements, and daily challenges.
  • To show public chat and public ranking information to other players.
  • To operate, protect, and improve the website.

3. Third-party services

The site uses Google Firebase services for authentication and realtime data features. Those services may process data according to Google's own terms and privacy documentation.

4. Advertising

WorldOrWrong may use advertising services, including Google AdSense, in the future. If advertising is enabled, ad partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver and measure ads. Any production advertising rollout should also include the corresponding consent and messaging configuration required for applicable regions.

5. Public content

Leaderboard names, scores, and public chat messages may be visible to other users. Do not post sensitive information in public areas.

6. Local storage

Your browser may store gameplay and interface preferences locally to keep the experience working between visits.

7. Children's privacy

The site is not intended for knowingly collecting personal information from children without appropriate legal basis or parental consent where required.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision.
